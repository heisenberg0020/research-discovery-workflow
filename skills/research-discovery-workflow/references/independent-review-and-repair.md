# Q5 and mandatory Q5-R: preserve, review, repair

## Q5 — Independent proposal and conditional plan

**Entry:** Q3/Q4 have produced concrete content or reached an honest bounded stopping point. Previous research results remain withheld.

1. Compose a standalone-readable independent proposal. For each developed candidate, preserve:
   - Research object and one clear claim.
   - Existing answer and exact place we seek to explain/change.
   - Remaining-opportunity consequence and its fact/inference/hypothesis status.
   - Actual mechanism: defining relationships, operations, objective, information timing and necessary details.
   - Serious alternative, meaningful distinguishing prediction/comparison, and conditions where the old answer may suffice.
   - Main risk and the unresolved kind of work.
   - Staged validation: claim, comparison, observable, result branches and required resources/access.
2. Keep the key mechanism in the main prose. Appendices may hold derivations and full controls, but the reader must not invent the essential process after reading the main report. Compare against Q3/Q4's original substantive paragraphs to catch content lost in compilation.
3. State separately work status, proposal formation, claim-specific evidence adequacy, and validation status. A defined untested method can be a completed proposal; a problem map with “find a mechanism later” cannot.
4. Save the **original independent Q5** in a distinct location such as `output/q5-original/REPORT.md`. Do not edit it during subsequent repair or merge. Existing output names can be used if the versions are clearly distinguished; do not duplicate every note simply to fill a directory.

**Exit:** Original snapshot preserved, independently formed content readable, and its actual completeness stated. Available decisive reading still undone means partial work; do not falsely mark Q5 scientifically complete. An honest partial snapshot can undergo review and final partial reporting; reconciliation may help only if authorized, without retroactive claims of complete independent ideation.

## Q5-R — Independent retrospective and paper-only repair

**Entry:** Original Q5 is saved. No previous research candidates, architectures, rankings, result packages or old experimental outputs have entered. This stage is mandatory even on first use.

### 1. Review what actually happened

Use the current pass's sources, first-route records, proposal versions and neutral goals. Prefer a fresh reviewer context receiving only that packet if supported. If only self-review is possible, state it; a named role or a new heading is not independent review.

Look at substance, not template completion:

- Did T/U really explore different reasoning routes? Did S align claims and change understanding, or merely merge summaries?
- Was the current answer understood, including stronger later treatments? Did caution become avoidance of hypotheses? Did a broad important problem become a concrete consequential opportunity?
- Did the important construction survive into Q5 main prose? Where did it become a vague wish, caveat or pointer?
- Which specific scientific interfaces are wrong or underspecified: objects/shapes, variables, derivatives, observability, timing, target population, objectives, algorithm identity, equivalence claims, or comparison conditions? Inspect only those relevant to the actual proposal, not a universal audit list.
- Do claimed differences genuinely produce different operations or predictions? Is the simplest serious explanation/control represented fairly?

### 2. Diagnose at the level of the claim

Write the few consequential findings as **location → concrete problem → why it matters → proposed repair**. Distinguish a prose omission, a mathematical/interface mistake, a known-operation misidentification, an unfair comparison, an evidence gap, and an empirical unknown.

Do not force defects to appear or redesign every idea. For example, if a proposal caches a transformed representation but reconstructs an untransformed one, write the compatible cache/reconstruction rule; do not add a disclaimer and leave the operator undefined. If a bounded-information equivalence argument actually generalizes, correct the boundary and reconsider what the study can test. These are hypothetical review examples, not suggested research topics or empirical results.

### 3. Repair on paper, preserving the original

Fix justified local problems in a separate revised version, such as `output/q5-repaired/REPORT.md`. Clarify definitions, operators, targets, controls and claims; rederive relevant relationships; reread decisive current-pass/public sources where necessary. Paper-only repair includes a symbolic or clearly constructed walkthrough, not simulation, data fitting or numerical effect testing.

If a local fix reveals a larger obstacle, return to Q3/Q4 with the same independent scope. Do not smuggle in old results, build a general platform, or claim a critical unresolved mechanism has been repaired. Record remaining issues and partial maturity. If no material correction is justified, record what was checked and why the proposal stands; no artificial edits are required.

### 4. Put a clear research core at the beginning

Open each repaired proposal with connected prose answering:

> **What is the existing answer? What do we still propose to discover or improve? What meaningful difference would make this a substantive research contribution?**

Immediately explain how the proposal operates or how the explanation would be distinguished. Do not reduce it to a title plus uncertainty. Retain full equations/controls later, and retain the key mechanism in the main text. Withdraw a specific novelty claim if warranted without automatically abandoning an important same-problem improvement.

### 5. Verify that repair worked

Read the repaired scientific content, not just the change log. Trace the actual relationship/operation; compare it with the original; check consequences, alternatives and distinguishing predictions still make sense. State what changed in our understanding, what did not, and which claims remain unvalidated. Do not assert complete repair merely because a revised file exists.

**Exit:** Original retained; retrospective and actual repair/core clarification completed and separately saved; substantive checks read; unresolved issues disclosed. A concise `REVIEW.md` plus revised proposal is sufficient. Without a developed mechanism, finish this stage as reviewed partial progress, not “all proposals valid.” Only now may authorized old materials enter Q6.
