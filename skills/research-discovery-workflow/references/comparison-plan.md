# Q7-A: four questions before the comprehensive insight report

## Purpose, entry and deliverable

After the independent repair and applicable reconciliation, turn each retained developed direction into an **executable comparison scheme with a resource assessment**. Complete this before Q7-B's comprehensive insight report. Do not write another broad survey, run an experiment by default, or force every rough/reserve idea into fake execution readiness.

Use the actual mechanisms and evidence already built. Perform targeted primary-source, appendix, code or resource-documentation checks only where they resolve one of the four questions. The deliverable can be a concise main-report section per direction, with detailed design notes linked. Required substance, not a new template count, defines completion.

## 1. Where does the nearest work's answer actually stop?

Identify the closest relevant answer and the serious competitor; these may be different works. Specify:

- The claim/object it answers and the mechanism or operation that provides its answer.
- Relevant theoretical assumptions/guarantee or experimental task, information, split, comparator and measurement.
- What its evidence does establish, what it does not, and why the boundary matters here.

Read the decisive original positions. Do not restart generic searches for “similar papers” or restate a topic map. Lack of testing under a condition is uncertainty, not proof of failure. A standard treatment may still be the correct strongest comparator even without a newer paper. Reuse rather than weaken it for convenient comparison.

## 2. What exact difference do we still want to answer?

Write the specific unresolved relationship or proposed operational difference, then articulate **two competing accounts** and what observation/argument separates them. For a method proposal, these may explain whether its expected effect comes from the targeted operation or an existing/simple treatment, capacity, information, objective, sampling or resource change. Do not impose an unrelated psychological/causal mechanism on every method.

For example, “a conditional operation uses genuinely helpful pre-decision information” versus “the same result follows from a static rule or extra capacity” requires a comparison matching the appropriate information and capacity, not only removing a module. This is an illustrative reasoning pattern, not a topic suggestion or finding.

Define treatments/controls, available information, estimand/outcome, relevant conditions, observable predictions and residual ambiguity. If both accounts predict the same output, improve the discrimination or admit the design cannot identify the distinction. If prior proof already excludes an account, state that rather than pretending it remains an equally plausible hypothesis. For theory, a discriminating derivation/counterexample can replace empirical measurement.

## 3. Which real resources can answer it?

Identify named data, models/method implementations, formal objects, reference systems or measurement apparatus as appropriate. Check the publicly available description and relevant eligibility, not merely that a download link exists. For an empirical comparison specify:

- Data/task/population coverage and the information actually available at each decision.
- Model/implementation version and which required operation it supports; known approximation or reproduction gaps.
- Split, development/tuning and final evaluation roles; temporal/entity separation when relevant; how comparison contamination is avoided.
- Access/license constraints, whether access is verified or conditional, and the minimum suitable scale. Do not read unrelated old projects to find convenient assets before authorized Q6.

State why these resources retain the original scientific object. An easier dataset must not silently replace an unavailable population, delayed-feedback condition or intervention. If a substitute is useful, say what narrower question it can answer and what remains untested. Previous assets may be reused only when authorized and qualified; no old experiment automatically resumes.

If new data collection is necessary, specify a concrete collection route, target fields/population, apparatus or access partner, timing, measurement and permission dependencies. Existing public data is not a mandatory requirement or a reason to abandon a consequential question. Distinguish a qualified conditional collection plan from an unspecified hope to obtain data; do not invent access, consent or an existing partner.

Estimate the execution envelope at a useful level: relevant sample/workload, compute/calls, elapsed time, memory/storage or data collection effort. Mark measured/documented values versus derived estimates and assumptions; give a range or workload formula when precise figures are not available. Include the serious comparator and repeat/uncertainty needs, not only the new method's cheapest run. Do not invent benchmark times, costs or resource access.

For non-empirical work, identify the formal setting, applicable prior results, candidate proof route, examples/counterexamples and proof/analysis tooling. Do not fabricate a dataset requirement just to fill the empirical fields.

## 4. What decision would the result change?

Connect distinguishable result patterns to specific research actions, for example:

- Continue the proposed method because its targeted difference has evidence under the intended conditions.
- Reposition as explanation/value research because the observation identifies a phenomenon but the new method adds little.
- Adopt an adequate simple/existing treatment and retain only a still-meaningful question.
- Revise a mechanism or choose a more suitable validation setting because the tested resources miss the phenomenon.
- Keep unresolved and improve measurement because the comparison lacks power or the relevant condition did not occur.

Do not equate an execution error, weak measurement or small noisy negative with the direction being dead. A valid contradiction still changes the exact claim it hits. A positive output also does not prove every explanation or general usefulness. These branches are a proposed decision rule, not reported experimental results.

## Exit: actual plan readiness

Read all four answers together. Another researcher should be able to implement the core comparison without inventing the control, replacing the study object, choosing an unspecified target or guessing the needed resource substrate.

State one of:

- **Executable within specified conditions:** core comparison and eligible resources identified; practical envelope justified. Planning readiness is not experiment authorization or a guaranteed result.
- **Conditional:** the scientific comparison is concrete and named resources are qualified, but specific access/compute/collection remains a declared dependency. State the exact condition and what an allowed fallback can/cannot answer.
- **Incomplete:** mechanism/control/observation or suitable resource is still unidentified. Return to targeted construction/reading, reselect with justification, or report the concrete blocker and partial progress. Do not certify Q7-A complete or start a fully completed Q7-B report merely because four headings were filled.

A conditional scheme can complete the four-question planning task while making its limits explicit. An invented resource, generic “use a public dataset,” or “try experiments to see” cannot. Once the substantive scheme is delivered, proceed to the comprehensive insight report; carry the plan's actual readiness into the final account.

Judge readiness against the **retained main research object**, not its easiest subproblem. A finished symbolic interface check does not complete an empirical benefit study whose resource substrate is still unidentified. Share those local insights as progress, but do not label the overall Q7-A or comprehensive Q7-B completed. A genuinely theoretical research objective can use formal resources; do not silently reclassify an empirical objective as theory to bypass the missing resources. Explain any substantive repositioning, what it preserves/abandons, and its fit to the user's goals; seek direction if it materially changes the requested objective.
