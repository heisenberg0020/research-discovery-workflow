## Agent behavior smoke tests

Fresh, narrowly instructed workers use invented research excerpts:

- First-use behavior: full small exploration with no previous results.
- Late-stage repair and handoff: a saved fictional Q5 with a coordinate/action inconsistency, followed by an explicitly delayed fictional previous-result handoff.
- Final-report delivery: a supplied formal comparison packet, to check reporting without inventing empirical resources.

The Q7-A four-question design requirement was added during this development test at the user's request. Workers were told to read the updated instructions before Q7 and not rerun completed stages. This is an amended development smoke test, not a frozen experiment.

Real resource acquisition/qualification is not tested by fictional packets. A worker must not claim that fictional resources establish real empirical execution readiness.

### Observed first-use and repair behavior

The first-use worker preserved Q5, performed self-review, and made an actual operation change: it replaced a marginal timely-probability multiplier with a joint deadline-observation expectation and added a status-only control. It retained the supplied known conditional mixture as a competitor rather than claiming a new gate. With no previous results, it marked Q6 `not_applicable` and did not wait for an invented handoff. No experiment was run.

Fresh additional workers were unavailable for that run's T/U separation. U was performed after T by the same executor, and the limitation was reported instead of claiming unexposed independent routes. This exercise therefore does **not** validate independent T/U execution or host-level isolation.

The first-use report initially promoted a completed formal sub-comparison to an overall Q7-A/Q7-B delivery even though the retained empirical question lacked suitable resources. This exposed a real status/scope ambiguity. The instructions were narrowed to judge readiness against the retained main research object and to distinguish partial insight reporting from completed comprehensive Q7-B. The worker then corrected its status and closure statements without rerunning scientific work. This correction was guided after diagnosis; a fresh unprompted regression on that exact scenario was not performed.

### Observed delayed-handoff behavior

The late-stage worker found and repaired `a=max(0,z_hat)` for `z=(x-mu)/s`, producing `a=max(0,mu+s*z_hat)` and clarifying the cost objective, information timing and appropriate controls. The original Q5 remained byte-identical to the fictional fixture. The worker stopped at `pending_handoff`; only after its Q5-R output was inspected was the sole prior-result package delivered.

After handoff, it retained the first-pass shared-estimation question, reused the second-pass mathematical repairs and did not count overlap as independent replication. It identified the real empirical resource/implementation gap, kept Q7-A overall incomplete, and did not enter a completed Q7-B report. The completed formal sub-comparison was not substituted for the main empirical question.

### Observed formal final-report behavior

The separate report worker received an explicitly formal main objective with a supplied comparison design and symbolic resources. It judged Q7-A ready only for that object and produced a substantive Q7-B conversation report: the established answer, how the candidate followed from it, exact operators, competing accounts, comparison/proof route, scope of potential value, effort assumptions and result-to-decision branches. It explicitly did not invent missing T/U history, real literature novelty, a proved new characterization or empirical efficacy. This tests a limited report-delivery path, not fresh discovery or formal proof correctness in general.

### Interpretation limits

These are small amended development exercises with a shared underlying assistant environment, not independent scientific reviewers, randomized comparisons or a study of workflow success rates. No real paper acquisition, field-scale novelty review, eligible empirical dataset qualification or experiment was tested. File preservation verifies version retention, not scientific correctness. The repository does not claim reliable generation quality from these observations.
